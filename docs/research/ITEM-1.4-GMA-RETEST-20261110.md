## Root causes and changes

1. The prior FFmpeg `fps` output was labeled with `frame_index × interval`; those labels were not guaranteed to equal the actual frame presentation time. It sometimes mixed an English on-screen GMA statement with a Tagalog spoken paraphrase and rejected the clip on lexical overlap. The statement is relevant and should not be called an unrelated ticker merely because it is in English.
2. Frame acquisition now seeks to explicit times at the midpoint of each of three equal clip sections and records the same seek time on each OCR region. Topic comparison also ignores broadcast titles and handles `cyber libel` versus `cyberlibel` as one topic term. It still requires substantial shared story terms and does not prove word accuracy.
3. OCR geometry excludes channel logos, clock, ticker and end-card text. In the first extraction retest, three frames yielded four retained lower-third regions and 25 rejected detections. The visible headline says `REKLAMONG LIBEL AT CYBERLIBEL`, while OCR read `REKLAMONG LIBEL AI CYBERLIBEL` at 23.53 s. That OCR error remains; no word was silently rewritten.
4. The first same-file retest before explicit seeking returned an extraction error after 140.76 s and made no prediction. An intermediate explicit-seek run sampling the beginning of each section produced a **review preview** after 115.901 s, with the original imperfect ASR and 112 characters of retained frame text. It missed the later English on-screen statement. The final uniform-midpoint same-file retest (`tmp/media-regression/original-retest-uniform-20261110.json`) sampled **11.52, 34.55 and 57.58 s**, returned the speech/frame disagreement error after **110.563 s**, and made **no prediction**. The audio ASR still contained the suspicious phrases above. Different frame choices and warm model caches prevent a speed-improvement claim.
5. In that final retest, OCR rejected 9 detections at 11.52 s, 7 at 34.55 s and 6 at 57.58 s. It kept the relevant lower-third at 34.55 s and the GMA Network's English statement at 57.58 s: “GMA Network has yet to receive a copy of the complaint. We will review the matter and comment accordingly upon receipt.” EasyOCR produced a minor trailing `~` punctuation error and separately read `GMA NETWORK`. The speech is in Tagalog, so only **11 of 24** salient OCR terms overlap with the ASR; the 0.458 overlap fails the conservative 0.70 gate. This is a cross-language comparison limitation, not proof that the quote is unrelated. The full OCR diagnostics are private at `tmp/media-regression/ocr-uniform-20261110.json`.
